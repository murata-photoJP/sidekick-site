This end date is not a fixed deadline; we may change it depending on the progress of development and testing or for other reasons. Any change will be announced on this page. During the Beta period we will update the Beta from time to time (for example, beta 1.01, beta 1.02). Updated Beta versions are subject to these same terms and the same planned end date; an update does not start a new Beta period.

We may change the Beta, or suspend or end its availability, without prior notice. The Beta is software under development; its features, displays and calculation results may differ in the official version.

4. No guarantee of calculation results

The Beta calculates and shows candidate locations and times for photographs based on the positions of celestial bodies such as the sun and the moon relative to terrain and buildings. The results are estimates based on publicly available terrain data, building data and planetary ephemerides; they do not guarantee how a scene will actually look or that a photograph can be taken. Atmospheric conditions, changes in visibility caused by trees, buildings or seasons, the date of the underlying data, and temporary obstructions are not included in the calculation. We do not guarantee that the composition shown by the calculation can actually be photographed.

5. Things you must check on site

The locations shown by the Beta are calculated candidates. The Beta does not determine whether a location can be reached; whether entry is permitted (including private property, restricted areas and dangerous places); traffic restrictions or parking; weather; or safety at night, in the mountains, on the coast or in winter. Please check these yourself. Whether to photograph, your safety, and compliance with laws are your own responsibility.

6. Your photographs

We do not restrict, as a condition of using the Beta, the personal or commercial use of photographs you plan or take with the Beta (restrictions imposed by the managers of a location and the rights of third parties are a separate matter).

7. Sharing a shooting plan by QR code

7-1. Sharing by QR code

QR codes and share URLs created by the Beta's "Share by QR" feature contain the exact latitude and longitude of the shooting location and the shooting date and time. Anyone who receives them can see that location as it is. If a QR image or URL is passed on — by forwarding, screenshots, posting on social media, printing and so on — the shooting location may reach people you did not intend. Do not share plans for places you do not want to disclose. We cannot stop the spread of a QR image or URL once it has been shared.

What this feature shares is the shooting plan (location, date and time, and calculation results), not the Beta program itself (for redistribution of the program, see Section 10).

7-2. Publishing to the QR Map

If you turn on "Publish to QR Map" in the Beta, a shooting plan is listed on the "Planner QR Map", where anyone can see it, when you save a share QR code or copy a share URL. The listing includes the exact latitude and longitude of the shooting location, the shooting date and time, and the subject. Whether to publish is your choice. For details of how this is handled, see 9-6 of the Privacy Policy.

Do not publish plans such as the following: plans for private property, places where entry is prohibited or restricted, or places you do not want to disclose; plans that infringe the rights or privacy of others; and plans that violate laws or public order and morals.

If we receive a report or otherwise consider it necessary, we may stop showing a listing or delete it.

We do not guarantee that listings will remain or be shown. The Planner QR Map may be changed, suspended or discontinued without notice (including when the Beta ends).

You can remove a listing from the Beta on the PC that published it. If you lose the management key (the record on your PC), you cannot remove the listing from the Beta. If you would like the listing to be deleted, please contact us at the address in Section 11. After checking the listing and other details, we may be able to respond to your request.

We are not responsible for people who see a published plan visiting that location. For checks on site, see Section 5.

8. Usage records

When you generate a QR code, the Beta records only anonymous count information — that "a QR code was generated" — and sends it to our server (see the Privacy Policy for details; what is sent when you choose to publish to the QR Map is separate, as described in 7-2). This recording is enabled by default and can be turned off at any time from the Beta's "Usage records" setting. While it is off, nothing is recorded.

Map and elevation tiles are fetched directly from the Geospatial Information Authority of Japan's servers by your PC while the Beta is running. We do not guarantee the continued availability, content or uptime of these external services; if an external service stops, some features of the Beta may become unavailable.
