Person

Ichiro Murata

村田 一朗
Photographer / Developer of Sidekick
He currently develops and publishes Sidekick, a series of Photoshop scripts for photographers. Based in Kamakura, Japan, he continues to shoot mountain and star landscapes — mainly in the Northern Japan Alps — and to teach photography courses and workshops.

Profile

Born 1964 in Tokyo. Graduated from the Department of Marine Engineering, School of Marine Science and Technology, Tokai University.

While still a student he joined the Takagi Laboratory at the University of Tokyo's Institute of Industrial Science as an external research student, working on extracting the movement of warm water masses from NOAA weather-satellite thermal infrared imagery. He presented the results at the Television Society of Japan's national convention in 1986. He then spent roughly twenty years at an electronics manufacturer designing ICs for consumer televisions.

He began shooting mountain photography in the 1990s and won the 35th Gakujin Photo Award for 1997. As a member of the Japan Alpine Photographers Association he has shown work in its group exhibitions. He went independent in 2006, and from 2010 expanded into star-landscape photography.

Alongside magazine writing and product reviews, sample images and CP+ talks for PENTAX / RICOH, and teaching photography courses and workshops, he has consistently built the tools that were missing for his own shooting and processing. In the 2000s he released and sold photo software on Vector, and in 2012 a magazine feature published both his compositing method and the program itself. The Sidekick series is a continuation of that habit.
