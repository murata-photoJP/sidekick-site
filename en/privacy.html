Privacy Policy

プライバシーポリシー

Last updated: March 2026

This page is an English translation of the original Japanese policy. If there is any inconsistency, the Japanese version prevails.

1. Basic Policy

Sidekick (“the Service”) respects users’ privacy and is committed to protecting personal information. This policy explains how the Service collects and uses information.

2. Information We Collect

The Service may collect the following information:

  • Your name and email address when you contact us
  • Information processed through our payment provider, Stripe, when you make a purchase (the Service does not retain your credit card information)
  • Website access logs, including IP address, browser information, and pages viewed

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information only for the following purposes:

  • Providing and supporting purchased products
  • Responding to inquiries
  • Analyzing website usage to improve the Service
  • Sending important notices, such as service changes and issue notifications

We do not use personal information for purposes other than those listed above.

4. Disclosure to Third Parties

Except where required by law, the Service does not provide collected personal information to third parties without the individual’s consent. The following services are used, and information is processed in accordance with their respective terms:

  • Stripe: Payment processing (Stripe Privacy Policy)
  • Google Drive: File delivery

5. Cookies

This website may use cookies for analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, although doing so may affect some features of the Service.

6. Storage and Management of Personal Information

We take appropriate security measures to store collected personal information. Personal information that is no longer needed will be deleted promptly.

7. Requests for Disclosure, Correction, or Deletion

If an individual requests disclosure, correction, or deletion of their personal information, we will respond within a reasonable period. Please contact us using the details below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may change this policy as necessary. Changes will be announced on this page.
