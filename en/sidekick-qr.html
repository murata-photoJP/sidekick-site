Sidekick QR ｜ by Sidekick Lab

Sidekick QR Connect a photo to the web.

A photo, and the URL of the web page you want people to see. From those two, Sidekick QR makes a single card image with a QR code. Scan the QR code on the card with a smartphone and that web page opens.

Download (Windows, free) See how it works

Version 1.0.1 · ZIP (about 25 KB) · no installation

What it does

Photo + URL → QR card

It makes an image with your photo, a QR code, a title and a description. The photo is not cropped: the whole photo is fitted into the card.

🖼️

Choose a photo

JPEG and PNG photos are supported. Portrait-orientation photos from a camera or a smartphone keep the right orientation.

🔗

Enter a URL

Any web page (http / https) — a portfolio, a blog post, a page about your work — becomes the QR code.

💾

Check it, then save

Check the finished card in the preview, then save it as a PNG image. What is saved is exactly the image you saw in the preview.

How it works

How to use it

Choose a photo

Choose one photo (JPEG / PNG) for the card.

Enter the URL, a title and a description

Enter the URL of the web page for the QR code, and the title and description to show on the card.

Make the card and save it

Check the preview and save it as PNG. Scanning the QR code with a smartphone opens the page you entered.

Local only

Your photo is not sent anywhere.

The photo, the URL you enter and the card you make are not sent to any external server for Sidekick QR's processing. The card is made on your own computer. There is nothing to upload and no account to create.

The QR code is also made on your computer, so the URL you enter is never passed to an online QR code service. A smartphone that scans a QR code on the card does, of course, open the web page that code points to.

Requirements and specifications

The release version for Windows.

Download

Sidekick QR 1.0.1 (for Windows, free)

Download (ZIP, about 25 KB)
  1. Download the ZIP.
  2. Right-click the ZIP and choose "Extract All".
  3. Double-click "Sidekick QR.html" in the extracted folder.

To finish, just close the browser tab.
